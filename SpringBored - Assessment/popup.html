Before you begin

AI-assisted assessment runner

Reads questions on the page, queries your selected Google Gemini model, and selects verified answers. It never submits an empty or failed attempt.

    Direct browser-to-Google requests

    Your Gemini API key is stored locally in browser storage and sent directly to Google's API endpoint.

  2. 02
    Verify critical results

    AI responses can occasionally be inaccurate. Use the live inspector and activity console to review answers.

    Your responsibility
