## 1. Educational & Research Purpose

**SpringBored** (`SpringBored/` and `SpringBored - Assessment/`) is an open-source software engineering project created to study DOM automation, cross-frame Manifest V3 browser extension architecture, and multimodal Large Language Model (LLM) reasoning on structured educational interfaces.

---

## 2. Academic Integrity & Institutional Honor Codes

By downloading, installing, or using either edition of **SpringBored**, you acknowledge and agree that:

1. **Course vs. Assessment Separation**: The standard **SpringBored (Course Edition)** is explicitly engineered to skip all quizzes, tests, and graded assessments so that evaluations remain untouched.
2. **User Responsibility**: You are **solely and exclusively responsible** for verifying that your use of `SpringBored` or `SpringBored - Assessment` complies with:
   - Your university, college, school, or employer's **Academic Honor Code** and student conduct policies.
   - The **Terms of Use** of Infosys Springboard (`infyspringboard.onwingspan.com`) and any partner assessment platforms.
3. **Prohibited Use in Proctored or High-Stakes Exams**: Do **not** use `SpringBored - Assessment` on proctored examinations, certification tests, or any assessment where external assistance or browser automation is prohibited by your institution or organization.
