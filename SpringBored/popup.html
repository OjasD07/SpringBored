Before You Begin

Local course automation

SpringBored automates video playback and reading checkpoints inside your browser, while leaving graded quizzes and assessments untouched.

  1. 01
    100% local execution

    No account credentials, tokens, or browsing data ever leave your device.

  2. 02
    Assessments are skipped automatically

    Quizzes, tests, and graded assignments are detected and bypassed so you can review them yourself.

  3. 03
    Your responsibility

    Use this tool in accordance with your organization or institution's policies.
